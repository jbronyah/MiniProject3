# Validation Reflection — jbronyah

I validated ten projects analyzed by Keoni Yandall (kyandall), comparing HasRecovered, WhyRecovered, ActivityPattern, Currentstatus and RecentThemes. Before discussion, we agreed on 36 of 50 comparisons (72.0%). Fourteen records differed: three WhyRecovered descriptions, four ActivityPattern labels and seven RecentThemes pairs. Our discussion resolved the four activity-pattern differences by accepting my labels and treated the three recovery-description differences as semantic differences. Agreement therefore increased to 43 of 50 comparisons (86.0%). The seven RecentThemes differences remain unresolved and are reported explicitly, rather than counted as successful adjudications.

The adjudicated activity labels emphasize long-term trajectories. ANTsR, scib and the WorldWide Telescope web client are classified as declining because their later activity remains substantially below earlier levels, even when occasional bursts or partial recoveries occur. Slang is rising: its annual totals increased from 1,212 in 2018 to 6,870 in 2024. This distinction clarified an interpretation pitfall: having commits in every month establishes continuity, but does not establish a steady commit rate. ActivityPattern agreement increased from 60.0% to 100.0%, with Cohen's kappa increasing from 0.4286 to 1.0.

Across several projects, long gaps followed periods of reduced activity or routine maintenance. MIRTK and rptR show substantial reductions from their earlier peaks, while the commits before other gaps contain ordinary fixes, documentation or packaging work without an explicit abandonment announcement. These signals describe the context of inactivity; they do not establish its cause. Gaps also did not necessarily mean project death. MIRTK, rptR and 3D-Wind-Field resumed work after gaps of 29, 36 and 41 zero-commit months respectively, although their post-gap activity was limited. Recovery after an earlier gap and current activity are separate judgments: MIRTK recovered in the supplied timeline but is still Inactive as at September 30, 2025.

Recovery was associated with concrete maintenance needs: rptR resumed tibble-related fixes and test/documentation updates; 3D-Wind-Field resumed CesiumJS/WebGL2 compatibility and CDN maintenance; scib resumed anndata2ri dependency maintenance. Our semantic resolutions retain these shared descriptions without claiming that the available commits prove why maintainers decided to return.

RecentThemes remains the largest source of disagreement, with agreement unchanged at 30.0%. The comparison mixes differences in sampling with differences in interpretation: the validator used historical default-branch commits, while the author's WoC export includes other branch records. These seven cases need a shared commit sample and explicit coding rules before a final theme pair can be agreed. Treating the two theme orders as equivalent prevents formatting differences from becoming artificial disagreements.

HasRecovered and Currentstatus both retained 100.0% agreement. The recovery comparison normalizes “Yes, but limited” to Yes and retains Slang's no-gap case as N/A. Cohen's kappa for recovery is undefined after excluding that N/A case because all nine eligible projects receive Yes from both raters; 100% observed agreement does not make kappa estimable when there is no category variation. Currentstatus kappa is 1.0000, with eight projects Active and two Inactive. Under the README, WhyRecovered and RecentThemes receive NA for kappa.
